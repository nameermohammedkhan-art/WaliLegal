Delete Your Wali Account

Last Updated: August 2026

Wali respects your privacy and gives you control over your personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your Wali account and associated data, you may request deletion at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Wali app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account (when available).
  3. Follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

Email: support@wali.ai

Subject: Delete My Wali Account

What Will Be Deleted

After verification, we permanently delete:

  • Your account information
  • Your profile
  • Your AI conversation history
  • Your saved preferences
  • Your personalized settings
  • Other personal data associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Legal obligations
  • Financial or tax compliance related to purchases

Any retained information is kept only as long as required by applicable law.

Deletion Timeline

Most deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
