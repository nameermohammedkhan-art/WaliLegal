Delete Your Wali Account

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Wali respects your privacy and gives you control over your personal information. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data, you may request deletion at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

Email us at:

walimuslimsupport@gmail.com

Use the subject:

Delete My Wali Account

Please send the request from the same email address used for your Wali account (if applicable) so we can verify ownership.

Anonymous Accounts

If you use Wali through Anonymous Authentication and have not linked your account to Google Sign-In, your account is not associated with an email address.

In this case, include any information that may help us identify your account. If we are able to identify it, we will permanently delete the associated data.

Data We Delete

  • Your Wali account
  • Your profile information
  • Your AI conversation history
  • Your saved preferences
  • Your personalization settings
  • Your journal entries and reflections
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention, security, financial reporting, or compliance purposes.

Deletion Timeline

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days.
